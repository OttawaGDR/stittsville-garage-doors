Why we started

The gap in this market is not “another garage door company.” It is the space between corporate chains that quote low on the phone and add fees on site, and unlicensed handymen who should not touch torsion springs. Stittsville deserves a third option: insured work, published starting prices, and a technician who already runs Jackson Trails to Bryanston Gate in a normal week.

Homeowners told us the same stories — a $69 service call that became $400 before the van opened, a “technician” who could not explain why a spring failed, or a three-day wait while a door hung open toward the golf course in Amberwood. We built this site and this service model to answer those frustrations directly: same-day windows when possible, a written quote on site, and copy that names your neighbourhood instead of “the Ottawa area.”

What we believe

Upfront pricing. Our service call fee is $89 and it covers diagnosis plus a written quote. If you approve the repair during the same visit, that fee applies toward the job. Starting prices for springs, openers, cables, and tracks live on our pricing page — not buried in a PDF.

No upsells. If a tune-up fixes the grinding on your Fairwinds door, we do the tune-up. If a single panel replacement saves a sound door in Crossing Bridge Estates, we do not push a full install. We explain what failed, what options cost, and what happens if you wait.

Fix it right the first time. Balancing a door after a spring swap, testing auto-reverse after sensor work, and torquing hardware to spec is not optional — it is how you avoid a second call when the opener burns out on the next -25°C morning near Saunders Farm country.
