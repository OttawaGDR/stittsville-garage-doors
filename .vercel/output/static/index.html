What to do before the technician arrives

A few small steps shave time off the visit and, on safety calls, keep the door from doing more damage before we get there. None of these require tools — and none ask you to touch a torsion spring, cable, or live opener wiring (please do not).

If a spring just snapped

Do not try to lift the door manually — it is now extremely heavy. Do not use the opener: it can burn out the motor or pull the carriage off the rail. Unplug the opener from the ceiling outlet, leave the door where it is, and call (613) 777-6401. We bring the right spring wire size for double-car Stittsville doors.

If the door is off-track

Stop using the opener immediately. Continuing to run it bends horizontal track and damages panels — a quick track realignment turns into a full section job. Block the door open with a stepladder under the roller carriage if it is sagging, keep kids and pets clear of the bay, and book a visit.

If the opener won't respond

Check the photo-eye sensors near the floor on each side — one steady light, one flashing usually means misalignment or a snowbank in the beam. Pull the manual release rope only if the door is fully closed, never when it is partway open. Note the opener brand and model number on the back of the unit before you call.

Clear the work area

Move vehicles out of the garage if it is safe to do so. Clear about three feet on each side of the door tracks. A clean floor in front of the door lets us cycle the door safely and check balance without anything in the way.

Have payment info ready

We accept e-Transfer, credit card, and cash. You see the written quote on site before any work begins — there is nothing to pay until you approve it. Seniors and veterans should mention the discount at booking so it is on the written quote, not added after.

Park-and-access notes
