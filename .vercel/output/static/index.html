Common Stittsville garage door problems

Four failures account for most of our calls between the Goulbourn Recreation Complex and the Trans Canada Trail access in Amberwood. Each has a mechanical cause, a Stittsville-specific trigger, and a service page with pricing and timing.

Broken spring (cold-snap failures)

When Ottawa drops below -20°C for a week, torsion springs on Fairwinds and Amberwood homes become brittle. Homeowners hear a bang in the garage, then the door feels impossibly heavy or hangs crooked on one cable. Extension springs on older pulley setups fail the same way after years of cycles. This is not a DIY fix — stored spring energy can cause serious injury. We replace broken springs same day when wire size is on the truck, balance the door, and test auto-reverse before you head back out toward Hazeldean. See garage door spring repair in Stittsville for starting prices and what the visit includes.

Opener will not respond (sensor misalignment after winter)

The motor hums but the door reverses immediately, or the wall button flashes and nothing moves. After snowbanks build up near the apron on Jackson Trails driveways, photo-eyes get knocked or frost over on north-facing bays in Wyldewood. Wi-Fi openers in Bryanston Gate lose pairing after router changes. We realign sensors, replace worn gears and carriages on LiftMaster and Chamberlain units, and reprogram remotes — usually in under 90 minutes. Details on our garage door opener repair page.

Door off track (impact damage)

A vehicle tap, hockey net, or bent bottom vertical can pull rollers out of the channel. The door may bind, gap on one side, or look like it is about to fall. Continuing to run the opener risks bending the horizontal track and damaging panels on three-car setups in Crossing Bridge Estates. We straighten or replace verticals, re-seat cables if needed, and swap rollers before the track is ruined. Read garage door track repair in Stittsville for process and pricing.

Loud grinding (worn rollers and bearings)

Steel doors on 1990s builds in Fairwinds and Amberwood often sound like a freight train when rollers dry out or hinges crack. The noise gets worse in winter when grease thickens and the opener strains against extra friction. Ignoring it burns out motor capacitors and can snap cables off the drum. A garage door maintenance and tune-up fixes most of it in 45 minutes — lube, balance, hardware torque, and safety tests — before you need a full opener replacement.
